# SignedOff

  **Permit tracking software that auto-syncs building permit status from official city portals.**

  Contractors, homeowners, architects, and solar installers get automated email alerts before permits expire — no more calling the planning department or        
  refreshing browser tabs.

  ### What we track
  - 12 jurisdictions across California, North Carolina, Florida, and Colorado
  - LADBS, EPIC-LA, and 9 Accela-powered city portals
  - Daily auto-sync with change detection

  ### Features
  - Smart permit detection — enter any permit number, we find the jurisdiction
  - Expiration reminders before deadlines hit
  - PDF reports with QR codes
  - Team sharing for up to 3 members
